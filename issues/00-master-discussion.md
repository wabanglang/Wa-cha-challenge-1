# [DISCUSSION] What if “broken society” is partly a social-infrastructure failure?

**Owner / originating author:** DOUGLAS W. T., JACKS0N

## Question

Can a meaningful share of addiction, self-harm, violent extremism, chronic instability, and social alienation be reduced by treating **belonging, dignity, agency, and durable local institutions as infrastructure** rather than as secondary “soft” concerns?

## Working model

```text
institution/economic loss
→ weaker social bonds + fewer legitimate roles
→ loss of dignity/agency/belonging
→ alienation
→ increased vulnerability to destructive coping or exploitative identity systems
```

That chain may be partly right, partly wrong, population-specific, bidirectional, or confounded. The purpose here is to make it testable.

## Proposed working thesis

**People are less likely to remain trapped in destructive systems when they have credible, accessible routes to belonging, agency, stability, contribution, and future status.**

This is a hypothesis, not a moral judgment and not an assertion that poverty or trauma mechanically cause violence or addiction.

## RWAL formulation

```text
CLAIM := SOCIAL-RUPTURE is a RISK-MULTIPLIER, not a single cause.
GOAL  := reduce PREVENTABLE-HARM without increasing coercion or stigma.
METHOD:= REAL{RECONNECT→ENABLE→ANCHOR→LEARN}.
TEST  := compare baseline→intervention→outcomes→harms→costs→persistence.
FAIL  := no meaningful improvement | harm↑ | coercion↑ | inequity↑ | cost unsustainable.
```
