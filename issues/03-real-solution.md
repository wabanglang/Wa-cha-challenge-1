# [SOLUTION] REAL — Reconnect → Enable → Anchor → Learn

**Owner / originating author:** DOUGLAS W. T., JACKS0N

## `DEF:REAL`

```text
REAL := R→E→A→L
R := RECONNECT{belonging, trusted-human-contact, family/peer/community ties}
E := ENABLE{housing, health, food, docs, mobility, income, work, skills, navigation}
A := ANCHOR{durable local institutions, ownership, routines, roles, places-to-belong}
L := LEARN{baseline, measurement, causal audit, harm audit, repair, replication}
```

## Why sequence it this way?

A person may not be able to use a job program while unsheltered, untreated, undocumented, or unable to travel. A service may stabilize someone temporarily without creating durable belonging. A neighborhood program may feel successful while producing no measurable outcome. REAL therefore treats **relationship, capability, institution, and evidence** as separate but connected layers.
