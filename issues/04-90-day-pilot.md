# [PILOT] Design a 90-day REAL neighborhood cell

**Owner / originating author:** DOUGLAS W. T., JACKS0N

## Objective

Design the smallest ethical, measurable field test of REAL that can fail clearly and cheaply before anyone proposes scale.

## Candidate pilot shape

```text
UNIT := one bounded neighborhood/service catchment
DURATION := 90 days
COHORT := small voluntary participant group with explicit eligibility rules
BASELINE := pre-intervention conditions + existing services
INTERVENTION := minimum-sufficient R + E + A components
LEARN := weekly process review + predefined outcome windows
```

This is a design discussion, not approval to enroll participants. Any real-world pilot must satisfy applicable ethical, legal, clinical, privacy, accessibility, and safeguarding requirements first.

## Minimum design questions

1. Who is the target population, and who is explicitly out of scope?
2. What existing service is the comparison/baseline?
3. Which *one or two* REAL bottlenecks are being tested rather than trying to solve everything?
4. What services already exist and should be connected rather than duplicated?
5. What outcome can plausibly change within 90 days?
6. What adverse outcomes trigger modification or stop?
7. What happens to participants when the pilot ends?
8. How is participation kept voluntary and non-punitive?

## Suggested stage gates

```text
G0: community/problem validation
G1: evidence + ethics review
G2: operational feasibility
G3: baseline captured
G4: limited launch
G5: midpoint harm/benefit audit
G6: 90-day outcome analysis
G7: STOP | REPAIR | REPEAT | EXPAND
```

## Success is not activity

Referrals, meetings, case notes, app usage, money spent, or people processed are implementation measures—not proof of improved human outcomes.

The pilot must be designed so a negative or null result is publishable and useful.
